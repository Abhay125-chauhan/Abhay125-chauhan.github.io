About Me

I am a computational materials scientist and chemist with research experience in molecular dynamics simulations, quantum chemistry, and computational modeling of molecular and nanoscale systems.


My research has focused on understanding structure, dynamics, thermoresponsive behavior, hydrophobic collapse, polymer conformations, and molecular interactions using atomistic and coarse-grained computational approaches.


I am particularly interested in developing computational approaches to understand complex molecular systems and connect molecular-level interactions with emergent structural and thermodynamic behavior.

Research Projects

Designing Thermoresponsive Nanoparticles through Hydrophobicity Control of Grafted Diblock Copolymers

M.S.(R.) Thesis — Indian Institute of Technology Delhi

This project investigated how polymer hydrophobicity and temperature influence the structure and conformational behavior of polymer-grafted nanoparticles.

  • Designed generic polymer-grafted nanoparticle models with 20-bead polymer chains.
  • Systematically varied polymer hydrophobicity from 0–100% in 10% increments.
  • Performed molecular dynamics simulations at 270, 300, 330 and 360 K.
  • Analyzed polymer–nanoparticle structure, inter-chain contacts, effective nanoparticle radius and polymer conformation.
  • Calculated free-energy / PMF profiles and generated two-dimensional density maps to investigate hydrophobic collapse and temperature-dependent conformational transitions.

Methods: Molecular Dynamics · GROMACS · Python · VMD · Free-Energy Calculations

MD-Guided Design of Hybrid Polymer–Protein Conjugates with Tunable Aqueous Solubility and pH-Responsive Polymer-Grafted Gold Nanoparticles

Research Project — Indian Institute of Technology Delhi

Molecular dynamics simulations were used to investigate polymer architecture, grafting density and pH-dependent structural behavior of polymer-functionalized gold nanoparticles.

  • Investigated PEG, PLL–PEG, PLL–PEG–PLL and PLL polymer architectures.
  • Performed DFT dihedral scans to determine PEG–PLL linkage parameters.
  • Developed PEG6–PLL6-functionalized gold nanoparticle models.
  • Varied grafting density from 4 to 40 polymer chains to investigate polymer morphology and the mushroom-to-brush transition.
  • Investigated pH-dependent structural changes in PEG6–PLL14 functionalized gold nanoparticles.

Methods: Molecular Dynamics · GROMACS · DFT · Python · VMD · Polymer Modeling

Pressure-Induced Polymerization of Carbon Monoxide: A Quantum Chemical Investigation of (CO)n Oligomerization

M.Sc. Thesis — Malaviya National Institute of Technology Jaipur

This project investigated the molecular mechanism of pressure-induced carbon monoxide polymerization using quantum chemical calculations.

  • Investigated (CO)n clusters with n = 2–6.
  • Generated structures using ABCluster and optimized them using density functional theory and coupled-cluster-level approaches.
  • Investigated CO clusters confined inside He@C60 and He@C180 cages to model pressure effects.
  • Used ONIOM-based computational models to simulate increasing pressure through cage-size reduction.
  • Analyzed structural, energetic, bonding, electronic and wavefunction properties to understand CO polymerization.

Methods: DFT · ONIOM · Quantum Chemistry · ABCluster · Gaussian · ORCA
